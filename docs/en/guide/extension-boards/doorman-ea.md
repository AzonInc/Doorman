# Doorman EA - Audio Extension <Badge type="tip" text="Only for Doorman S3 >= 2.0" />

The **Doorman EA Audio Extension** board adds full audio capability to your Doorman S3. It allows you to both listen and transmit audio back through the intercom line.

The board is built around the [ES8388](http://www.everest-semi.com/pdf/ES8388%20DS.pdf) audio codec, providing high-quality analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog conversion for reliable two-way audio communication.
